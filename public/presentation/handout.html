Front Door 🏡

Free, plain-language help for working families buying a home in California, starting with Orange County. Answer a few questions and get the loans and down payment help that fit your family, in six languages, with no sign-up.

😟 The problem

10 : 1OC home price to household income (≈4:1 is affordable)
$1.3M+Typical Orange County home (Habitat OC 2025 Impact Report)
$335KYearly income needed to afford it at 7.18%, plus $260K down
3 of 5Example households can't afford it, including a teacher + nurse earning $210K

✨ What Front Door does

  • A friendly check-in → a real plan: income group (2026 limits, all 58 CA counties), credit history, job & employer, groups, disability, ITIN/self-employed.
  • Matched programs with honest status labels: grants, city loans, CalHFA, Habitat, VA/FHA, private and mission lenders.
  • Where to borrow: lenders sorted by the help they accept and what OC buyers actually paid (HMDA 2024).
  • Calendar + ready-to-send emails, a personal report with a 12-month plan, and a printable readiness packet.
  • Learn in plain words: rates, every kind of mortgage, new CA laws, an interest saver, and "Five buyers, one market."

🧱 Lane 3: Fund it

  • Fund-the-gap calculator: derives the family's price from CA Health & Safety Code §50052.5 and balances cost = family + subsidies + earned income + fundraising.
  • Community Down Payment Fund: residents, companies, and employers pool tax-deductible gifts in a nonprofit (test-mode donate page).
  • Rate and delay sensitivity show how quickly the gap grows.

🛡️ Trust

  • Every fact cited; programs labeled verified, unverified, or closed.
  • verify:docs checks 2,784 income-limit numbers and every quoted figure against the source PDFs.
  • A daily Haiku check flags program and rate changes in a pull request for human review.

🤖 How it was created

  • Built in one day (Nov 7, 2026) with Claude Code, Anthropic's AI coding agent, directed by our team.
  • Parallel research agents checked live official sources: programs, down payment subsidies, cheapest lenders, and new CA laws (5 agents, ~683K tokens, 319 tool calls).
  • Facts pulled from the hackathon PDFs with pdftotext; every page tested with lint, build, and headless-Chrome screenshots on phone and desktop.
