Lane 3 · Fund it

Make every home balance to $0

🧮 Fund-the-gap calculator

Family price from CA H&S Code §50052.5 + 2026 AMI + today's rate. Cost = family + subsidies + earned income + fundraising. Delay and rate sliders show how fast the gap grows.

💛 Community Down Payment Fund

Residents, companies, and employers pool tax-deductible gifts in a nonprofit; grants go to families by fair rules. Donate page ready in test mode.
